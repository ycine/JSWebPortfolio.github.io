I’m a Python developer with a background in GIS and photovoltaic design. I focus on building automation, backend tools and AI‑driven workflows that solve practical problems and eliminate repetitive tasks. My work often bridges spatial data, web applications and system‑level integrations.

I studied Geoinformation at the University of Łódź and have worked across water and sewer network mapping, enterprise database management, Python automation and SaaS‑based web GIS platforms. I also contributed to renewable energy projects as a photovoltaic designer for both small systems and commercial installations.

In addition to GIS and PV work, I’ve developed EMS software components and integrated industrial devices using Modbus, MQTT and TCP/IP. I build tools for monitoring, diagnostics and data processing in energy systems, and continue to explore LLMs, agents and workflow orchestration through personal projects in Python and JavaScript
